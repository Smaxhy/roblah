# v0.13: what each request became

The request: a complete overhaul of the midgame and endgame, with far less repetition, varied
lore-accurate events, dungeons that aren't the backbone and don't pop a menu, much better UIs,
higher stats, real goals and very hard things to reach, many more items and buffs, money that
means something, marriage, rivals who become friends, turf wars, and a more engaging story.
Engine code is in `src/shared/Engine/`, the City pack in `src/shared/City/`, the screens in
`src/client/UI/`. `tests/v13_check.luau` checks most of it.

| Request | What it is now | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Events repeat; it feels like a loop | A card already seen in this life gets rarer each time (40%, 16%, 6%...); jobs come with **twists** (a client who lied, a rival on the same job, rain, an audit, a tip...); activity lines rotate year to year | `Engine/Sim.luau` (eventWeight), `Engine/Jobs.luau`, `City/Jobs.luau` (twists) |
| Genuinely different, lore-accurate events: L Corp facilities, abandoned branches, abnormalities, remnants, strange City events | **16 episodes**: multi-card stories that happen once, chain card to card (sometimes years apart), and end three or four different ways. A buried branch's intercom still paging an agent; a TETH unit behind a flooded wall and the four kinds of work; the L Corp agent next door; an energy truck on its side; a Library book with a Fixer inside it; a fallen Wing's medal; supper with a Bloodfiend Family; a Workshop's masterwork; your Office on fire; a debt to the Middle; someone from past the wall; an hour T Corp lent you; a streetlight that follows you; thirteen umbrellas; the last contract; an apprentice | `City/Episodes.luau`, `City/Events/Episodes_LCorp.luau`, `Episodes_City.luau`, `Episodes_Legacy.luau` |
| Memorable without leaning on famous characters | Every person in the new content is this game's own; canon is the setting (L Corp's work types, risk classes, Qliphoth counters, the Library's books, Hana, the Fingers), never a cameo | the files above |
| Dungeons shouldn't be the backbone; no menu popping up | Ways in are **found in the world** as a card (a hatch, a lift, a letter): go in now (the descent plays out as cards, no action spent), remember the way, or leave it. The DO tab lists only **Places you know** | `City/Entrances.luau`, `City/init.luau`, `Engine/Sim.luau` (startExpeditionInline), `client/UI/DoTab.luau` |
| Dungeons varied in purpose and structure | 8 new delves, each with its own purpose: exploration, survival, investigation, escort, salvage, negotiation, escape, compliance; drawn as a descent with a depth gauge | `City/Expeditions/Delves.luau` |
| A midgame with its own identity | **Turf wars** (Backstreets, 18+): join under your own name or the Thumb's, the Middle's or the Kurokumo Clan's colours; take blocks (the fight is with whoever holds the block), walk them, collect in person; raids, parleys, your side's pushes; hold every block and a Finger notices. **Cases**: five investigations worked a clue at a time (each with its own stat), with finales that close them different ways | `Engine/Turf.luau`, `City/Turf.luau`, `Events/Turf_Wars.luau`, `Activities/Turf.luau`; `Engine/Cases.luau`, `City/Cases.luau`, `Events/Cases.luau` |
| An endgame with its own identity | **Phases** of a life (Childhood, Youth, Making a Name, Power, Legacy) with a card when each begins; **legacy** content: the last contract, an apprentice who can carry your name on, a seat at Hana's table, an endowment for the District, memoirs | `City/Phases.luau`, `Events/Episodes_Legacy.luau` |
| Higher stats; very hard things to reach | Stats can now go **past 100**: renown grades raise the limit (+5 to +40), feats and deeds break limits, legendary gear and full sets raise them; tiers **VI and VII** with new perks. Past 90 a gain lands one time in four, so 110+ is a long life's work | `Engine/Body.luau` (statCap, capBonus), `City/init.luau` (perks, renown caps) |
| Actual goals | **47 feats**, bronze to mythic (an Urban Nightmare's grade, every block of a District, a million Ahn, every stat at 100, a stat at 130, a fifth generation...), with progress bars; gold and mythic ones break limits | `Engine/Feats.luau`, `City/Feats.luau`, `client/UI/Goals.luau` |
| Many more items, buffs and weapons, better organised | **60 new items**: 25 one-of-a-kind finds from delves, episodes, turf and cases; 21 Workshop pieces in 7 **sets** (2- and 3-piece bonuses); 6 big-ticket legendaries; 10 small useful things. A **stash** keeps what you replace: equip it back, or sell it | `City/Gear/Found.luau`, `City/Gear/Arsenal.luau`, `City/GearSets.luau`, `Engine/Sim.luau` (stash, sets) |
| Money that means something | Legendary gear at 12,000 to 26,000 Ahn; endowments of 10,000+; a turf war's tribute and pay; a case finale that costs 6,000; selling finds | as above |
| Getting married | A household can become a **marriage** at the District office (an interaction once the bond is high). Never romance, never another player. `Config.marriage = false` turns it off; households stay | `City/init.luau` (hold_wedding, pack.marriage), `Engine/Effects.luau`, `Config.luau`, `Pack.luau` |
| Becoming friends with your rivals | **Bury the hatchet** (an interaction), **settle it in the yard** (a yearly bout), and rival events (their bad year, the same contract, an enemy you share) that can end the rivalry for good | `City/init.luau`, `Events/Rivals_Household.luau` |
| Much better UIs | Event cards dressed for what they are (episode band with progress pips; terminal, descent, blood, library, street, smoke, strange, nest, lake and gold papers; a RARE ribbon); the **Arsenal** (framed rarity tiles, sets, limits, stash) and the **Workshop** (tabs by slot and set, grouped by rarity); the **Goals** sheet (phase, ambitions, cases, episodes, feats board); a **turf map** on the CITY tab; phase, cases and episodes on the STORY tab; job twists on the jobs board; feats, phases and episode endings celebrated | `client/UI/EventCard.luau`, `Theme.luau`, `Arsenal.luau`, `Goals.luau`, `SidePanel.luau`, `DoTab.luau`, `Feed.luau`, `App.luau` |

## Not tested in Roblox Studio

was run inside Roblox Studio for this version.
