# Solo Leveling Canon Brief (manhwa-first)

**Scope:** Canon = webtoon (Chugong / DUBU, Redice Studio / h-goon), main story ch 1–179, side story ch 180–200. Chapter numbers are webtoon unless marked "novel" (novel numbering differs; e.g. Red Gate = novel 56–64). These are kept out of canon: anime-only material, *Solo Leveling: Arise* (game), *Ragnarok* (sequel, a separate continuity).

**Method caveat:** My environment blocked direct page fetches, Fandom included. I cross-checked facts through search-indexed Fandom text and independent sites (CBR, Game Rant, Screen Rant, Beebom, Sportskeeda, ComicBook.com, Wikipedia). I did not read chapters directly. Check anything [LIKELY] or lower against the chapter before hard-coding it.

## 1. Gates
- First appeared about **10 years** before Jinwoo's first scene. [CONFIRMED] (matches the ~10-year epilogue reset)
- Calendar year: **NO CANON VALUE**.
- Six ranks, E<D<C<B<A<S, the same letters as Hunter ranks. [CONFIRMED]
- The Association measures a Gate's mana with equipment and ranks it. [LIKELY] Numeric thresholds per rank: **NO CANON VALUE**.
- Ordinary Gates are drawn blue. There is no colour-per-rank scheme; only Red Gates are named by colour. [LIKELY] Colour tiers and purple "Dungeon Break" gates are Arise mechanics. [CONFIRMED as game]
- **Dungeon Break:** an uncleared Gate releases its monsters. [CONFIRMED] The usual figure is **7 days**. [LIKELY] But the wiki says the Tokyo S-rank Gate broke about 3 days after spawning, and the novel forecasts that break for "the next day", so treat 7 as typical, not fixed. [UNCERTAIN/CONFLICTING]
- The Gate closes about **1 hour** after the boss dies. [LIKELY] (fan consensus; no chapter found; one guide says immediately)
- **Mana crystals** (also "magic crystals", "mana stones", "essence stones") come from monster bodies and mined deposits. They are Hunters' main income, an energy source and crafting material. [LIKELY]
- Electricity fails inside dungeons, so mining is done by hand with pickaxes. [LIKELY]
- Guilds run mining/porter crews; Jinwoo works as a porter on the Hunters Guild's A-rank orc Gate. [LIKELY]
- Loot and gear trade on the **Korean Hunters Auction**, which is separate from the Association. [LIKELY]
- **Recognition process:** detect, measure and rank the Gate, then sell raid rights to guilds or parties. Big guilds can be ordered to take emergency Gates (in return for tax breaks). [LIKELY]
- **Entry rules:** no party-size cap or "closes at party count" rule (**NO CANON VALUE**). Exceptions: Red Gates seal once entered; System instant dungeons are Jinwoo-only. [LIKELY]

## 2. Red Gates
- Looks normal, turns red once the party is inside, and the exit vanishes. No one gets in or out until the boss dies (or the Gate breaks), so outside help is impossible. [CONFIRMED]
- Time: about **1 day inside = 1 hour outside**. [LIKELY] (several sources agree; chapter not pinned)
- Readings mislead because the pre-entry reading reflects the disguise. The canon case read C and was at least B (the wiki says A). A Red Gate can't be re-measured. [UNCERTAIN/CONFLICTING on true rank]
- Survival is described as very poor (cold, no resupply); odds: **NO CANON VALUE**. [LIKELY]
- **Incident** (ch 46–55): a White Tiger Guild rookie training raid (Han Song-yi, A-rank Kim Chul) enters a C-rated Gate that turns red. Inside: an ice world with ice bears and ice elves led by boss **Baruka**. Jinwoo clears it and extracts Kim Chul as "Iron" and the alpha ice bear as "Tank". [CONFIRMED] Baruka's rank: [UNCERTAIN]
- A second Red Gate is reported later ("Recruitment" arc), details thin. [UNCERTAIN]

## 3. Double Dungeons
- **Cartenon Temple:** a hidden second dungeon inside a **D-rank** Gate (ch 1–10). [CONFIRMED]
- 17 entered and about 6 survived (Jinwoo, Lee Joohee, Song Chi-yul, Kim Sangshik, Kang Jeongho, and others). [LIKELY]
- Commandments, roughly: worship god, praise god, prove your faith. Statues kill rule-breakers. [LIKELY] (first rule's wording confirmed; the others paraphrased)
- It was a trial built by the **Architect** (the System's designer) to find Ashborn's vessel; Jinwoo becomes the "Player". [CONFIRMED]
- Jinwoo returns at level 100 (ch 123–131) and defeats the Architect. [LIKELY]
- The name "Kandiaru" for the Architect comes from *Ragnarok*. [LIKELY]
- Extremely rare: Cartenon is the only named canon Double Dungeon (it appears twice). [LIKELY]

## 4. Dungeon Breaks and incidents
- **Kamish:** the first S-rank Gate, US West Coast, about **8 years** before the story. The dragon devastated the region; reportedly 100+ Hunters fought and 5 survived, becoming the National Level Hunters. [LIKELY] Casualty figures: [UNCERTAIN] The fang was made into the daggers "Kamish's Wrath", later given to Jinwoo by Thomas Andre. [LIKELY]
- **Jeju:** an S-rank Gate appeared about **4 years** before the story. The first raid failed, then a Dungeon Break, and ants overran the island, which was abandoned. [CONFIRMED]
- **3 failed Korean raids** in total; the third, about 1 year before the story, killed S-rank mage Eunseok. [LIKELY]
- **4th raid** (~ch 90–107): Korea–Japan joint raid, **6 Korean + 10 Japanese S-ranks**, launched because winged ants threatened the mainland. Japan's Association secretly plotted the Korean S-ranks' deaths. About **8 S-ranks died** (7 Japanese incl. Goto Ryuji, plus Korean healer Min Byung-gu). Jinwoo killed the Ant King and extracted it as **Beru**. [CONFIRMED event; LIKELY counts]
- **Japan crisis** (ch 132–139): weeks after Jeju, an S-rank Gate opens in Tokyo/Shinjuku. Japan hires foreign S-ranks; barrier specialist Yuri Orloff dies when his barrier fails; **giants** break out. Jinwoo kills them and hidden boss **Legia**. [LIKELY]
- **Seoul** (~ch 153): the largest Gate ever appears over Seoul, takes days to open, and causes national panic. It is part of the Monarchs' war. [LIKELY] What emerged: [UNCERTAIN]
- **Final battle** (ch 167–177): Antares' armies invade and Hunters worldwide fight. [LIKELY] Antares reportedly emerges from a Gate in Canada. [UNCERTAIN]

## 5. Awakening
- Some people gain mana and abilities and become Hunters. It looks random and can happen at any age. [LIKELY] Age range: **NO CANON VALUE**.
- "Late awakener" as a formal rule: **NO CANON VALUE**.
- Population share: fan figures conflict (0.001%, 1 in 1,000, ~50k in Korea). [UNCERTAIN/CONFLICTING]
- Rank is normally fixed; **reawakening** is extremely rare, and the Association assumed Jinwoo had reawakened. The Rulers' vessels had induced second awakenings. [LIKELY]
- Hunters register with their national Association, are tested and get a **licence**. [LIKELY]
- **Reassessment:** the device can't read Jinwoo's mana, and he is declared **Korea's 10th S-rank** (ch 78), the first since Cha Hae-In (9th) two years earlier. [CONFIRMED]
- Jinwoo is first known publicly as "the weakest Hunter of all mankind". [CONFIRMED]
- **Classes:** Fighter, Mage, Assassin, Tanker, Ranger, Healer. [LIKELY] (used consistently, not chapter-pinned)
- Jinwoo's hidden job: Necromancer, later Shadow Monarch. [CONFIRMED]
- Arise roles (Breaker, Striker, Supporter...) and elements are game inventions. [CONFIRMED]
- **Eternal Sleep:** an incurable disease Jinwoo's mother has, cured with Holy Water of Life from the Demon Castle. [LIKELY]

## 8. Monarchs, Rulers, cosmology
The nine Monarchs (title; race ruled):
- **Ashborn:** Shadow Monarch, King of the Dead (shadows). [CONFIRMED]
- **Antares:** Monarch of Destruction, King of Dragons; strongest, final villain. [CONFIRMED]
- **Querehsha:** Monarch of Plagues, Queen of Insects. [CONFIRMED]
- **Rakan:** Monarch of Fangs, King of Beasts. [CONFIRMED]
- **Baran:** Monarch of White Flames, King of Demons. [CONFIRMED]
- **Yogumunt:** Monarch of Transfiguration, King of Demonic Spectres. [CONFIRMED]
- **Legia:** Monarch of the Beginning, King of Giants. [CONFIRMED]
- **Tarnak:** Monarch of Iron Body, King of Monstrous Humanoids. [LIKELY]
- **Sillad:** Frost Monarch, King of the Snow Folk (ice elves). [CONFIRMED]

What happens to each:
- Legia is killed by Jinwoo in Tokyo. [LIKELY]
- Rakan, Querehsha and Sillad attack Jinwoo together (~ch 158); Querehsha dies. [LIKELY]
- Rakan and Sillad die after Jinwoo takes Ashborn's full power (~ch 164). [LIKELY]
- Tarnak is killed in the final battle by Thomas Andre fighting alongside the shadows. [LIKELY]
- Antares dies in the final battle, but sources disagree on whether Jinwoo or the Rulers struck the final blow. [UNCERTAIN/CONFLICTING]
- Demon Castle Baran: the real Monarch or a System copy? Sources conflict. [UNCERTAIN/CONFLICTING]
- Yogumunt's fate: [UNCERTAIN]

Rulers and cosmology:
- **Rulers:** beings of light ("Fragments of Brilliant Light"). The Absolute Being created both Rulers and Monarchs, and wikis say the Rulers later overthrew it. [LIKELY]
- **Ashborn** was originally the strongest Ruler. As Shadow Monarch he later turned on the other Monarchs. He backed the Architect's System to raise a vessel, then *gave* Jinwoo his power instead of taking him over. [LIKELY]
- **Vessels:** the Rulers act through human hosts (sources say seven): Go Gunhee, Christopher Reed, Thomas Andre, Liu Zhigang, Siddharth Bachchan, a Brazilian Hunter (Jonas), and Sung Il-hwan ("envoy" per some). [UNCERTAIN on list]
- The Monarchs hunt the vessels and kill Reed, the Brazilian and Go. [LIKELY]
- **Sung Il-hwan** (Jinwoo's father) vanished in a dungeon about 10 years earlier. He returns during the war (ch 166) and dies after burning out his body. [LIKELY]
- **Cup of Reincarnation:** a Rulers' artifact that turns back time. [CONFIRMED]
- **Shadow Army:** Jinwoo raises the dead ("Arise"), with limited tries per corpse. [LIKELY] Key shadows: Igris (Job Change knight), Iron, Tank, Tusk (high orc shaman Kargalgan), Beru, Kaisel (wyvern), Greed, Bellion (Ashborn's grand marshal). [LIKELY]

## Do not state as canon
- Gates colour-coded by rank (the colour tiers are Arise mechanics).
- Every Gate breaking after exactly 7 days.
- Any calendar year for the first Gates or the story start.
- Exact awakening percentages, or exact Red Gate odds or time ratio.
- "Antoine Martinez" as the fifth National Level Hunter; "Kandiaru" as the Architect's name.
- Ma Dongwook leading the Fiend Guild (he leads Fame).
- Arise roles, elements, its Busan Gate story or game-only events.
- Jinwoo officially made a National Level Hunter.
- The Demon Castle Baran being definitely the real Monarch.
- Anyone besides Jinwoo, Cha Hae-In included, remembering the old timeline.
- "Kamish's Wrath" as the name of an incident (it is the daggers).
- *Ragnarok* events as part of the main series' world state.
