# Undermere: starter setting bible

An original setting that ships with the kit to demo every system. Replace it freely.

## The pitch
Undermere is a city built down the inside walls of a crater around a lake called **the Mere**.
The old city lies drowned at the bottom. Every few years the water drops (**Low Water**), the
drowned streets surface, and people go down to salvage what's left. Every few years it rises
(**High Water**) and the bottom tier floods. Whoever controls the floodgates controls who gets wet.

The tone is grim, wry and humane. The city grinds people down, and people are kind to each other
anyway. Think soot, pump-steam, lantern light on black water, tram bells, wet stone, kelp
broth, rope burns, old brass.

## Wards (districts) and their two sides
Undermere is split into **ten Wards**, wedge-shaped slices of the crater. Each Ward is
chartered to a company that holds a monopoly. The company's walled enclave, **the Charter**
(side `charter`), sits up on the sunlit Rim. The **Gutters** (side `gutters`) spill down the
Terraces to the Silt at the waterline. So *Rim*, *Terraces* and *Silt* are geography inside
every Ward: Charter folk live on the Rim, and Gutter folk live on the Terraces and the Silt.
Most people are born in the Gutters. Bad luck finds everyone, but it finds the Gutters far
more often.

## Old tier names (now geography)
- **the Rim**: sunlit gardens at the crater's lip. Private guards, glass greenhouses, people who
  have never been wet. Expensive to live in. Safe, but it eats your soul.
- **the Terraces**: the middle tiers. Switchback stairs, tram lines bolted to the
  rock, tenements, workshops, markets under tarps. Most people live here.
- **the Silt**: the bottom, at the waterline. Stilt-houses, salvage docks, rope
  bridges, fog. Cheap, dangerous, and it floods in High Water years.

## World state
- `water`: `low` / `normal` / `high`, rolled year to year.
  - **Low Water**: the drowned city surfaces. Salvage booms. Things come up with it.
  - **High Water**: the Silt floods. Displacement, disease, rescue work, opportunists.

## Powers & factions (careers)
- **Pumpworks** (`stoker`): the great pumps that keep the Terraces dry. Hot, loud,
  honest work. Ranks: Ash Hand → Stoker → Valve Keeper → Chief Stoker.
- **Lantern Guild** (`diver`): licensed salvage divers who go into the drowned streets with
  lanterns and rope. Brave, superstitious, paid in danger. Ranks: Wet Lantern → Diver → Deep
  Diver → Lantern → Lantern-Captain. Guild saying: *"A lantern goes down, a lantern comes up."*
- **Sluice Authority** (`sluice`): the bureaucracy that runs the floodgates and, really,
  the city. Clerks, inspectors, engineers. Paperwork decides who floods. Ranks: Junior Clerk →
  Clerk → Gatewarden → Engineer → Commissioner.
- **Brine Kin** (`brine`): a smuggling family in the Silt that runs boats through the
  drowned streets. They call each other cousin, and they keep debts in a ledger and on their
  faces. Ranks: Runner → Boatman → Ledger-hand → Cousin → Elder Kin.
- **Choir of Still Water** (`choir`): a quiet faith that believes the drowned city is
  listening. Members sit in long silences by the water. Mostly gentle, sometimes unsettling.
  Ranks: Listener → Chorister → Cantor → Still Voice.

## Strange things
- **The drowned city:** street signs under water, a cathedral roof breaking the surface at
  Low Water, rooms still set for dinner, music boxes that still play.
- **Creatures:** silt eels (long, pale, bite), lamp-eaters (drawn to light, they snuff
  lanterns), "the Quiet" (people-shaped shapes standing in the deep water, never seen moving).
  Keep them eerie, never gory.
- **Tidetouched** (form `tidetouched`): people who spend too long in the Mere change. Cool
  skin, slow breath, faint gill-lines, comfortable underwater. The Rim's gates won't let
  them through. Some are proud of it.
- **The breaking point:** when a mind hits bottom (sanity −45), it either **goes under**
  (becomes one of the Drowned, and the life ends) or **Surfaces** (form `surfaced`, stronger
  ever after).
- **Lantern Night:** once a year the city floats paper lanterns for the dead. When a life
  ends, it becomes a lantern in the player's Lantern Ledger.

## Currency
**scrip**, paper notes stamped by the Sluice Authority. Scale: a Silt kid finds 5–20; a
stitcher visit costs 30; a Wet Lantern earns 50/yr and a Lantern-Captain 650/yr; living on the
Rim costs 250/yr.
